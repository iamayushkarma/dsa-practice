SECTION 1 — MINDSET RULES (READ EVERY MORNING)
================================================================
1. Patterns > Count. Master 15-20 patterns deeply.
2. Never copy-paste a solution. Struggle for at least 30 mins.
3. After every problem ask: Why this approach? What pattern? Where else?
4. Revision is not re-reading. Re-solve from scratch.
5. A understood 200 > blindly done 400.
6. Take mock interviews. DSA alone won't get you hired.
7. Your goal is not rank. Your goal is a job.
