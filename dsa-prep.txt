SECTION 1 — MINDSET RULES (READ EVERY MORNING)
================================================================
1. Patterns > Count. Master 15-20 patterns deeply.
2. Never copy-paste a solution. Struggle for at least 30 mins.
3. After every problem ask: Why this approach? What pattern? Where else?
4. Revision is not re-reading. Re-solve from scratch.
5. A understood 200 > blindly done 400.
6. Take mock interviews. DSA alone won't get you hired.
7. Your goal is not rank. Your goal is a job.

SECTION 8 — NON-DSA PREP (Don't ignore this)
================================================================
This is what most people forget and fail because of:

1. PROJECTS (Very Important)
   - Have 2 solid projects you can explain end-to-end
   - Be ready: "What problem did it solve? What tech? What was hard?"
   - GitHub should be active and clean

2. CS FUNDAMENTALS
   - OOPS: Inheritance, Polymorphism, Abstraction, Encapsulation
   - DBMS: Joins, Normalization, Indexing, Transactions
   - OS: Process vs Thread, Deadlock, Memory Management
   - CN: TCP/IP, HTTP vs HTTPS, DNS basics

3. HR ROUND PREP
   Common questions for freshers:
   - Tell me about yourself (prepare 90-second answer)
   - Why this company?
   - Biggest strength/weakness
   - Where do you see yourself in 3 years?
   - Describe a challenge you overcame

4. RESUME
   - Keep it 1 page
   - List skills honestly (don't put things you can't answer)
   - Put LeetCode profile if 200+ solved
   - Quantify project impact if possible

================================================================
SECTION 9 — WEEKLY CHECKPOINT QUESTIONS
================================================================
Ask yourself every Sunday:
1. How many problems did I solve this week? (Target: 28-35)
2. Did I revise properly or just move forward?
3. Which pattern am I weakest in right now?
4. Did I write down my mistakes?
5. Am I understanding or just completing?

If answer to Q5 is "just completing" — slow down immediately.
